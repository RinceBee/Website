Areas of Interest

Land Development Engineering

Land development engineering transforms raw land into sites ready for construction. It brings together drainage, earthworks, road design and essential services. I enjoy the work because it combines technical problem-solving with practical design, turning ideas and plans into real developments.

Hydrology and Hydraulics

Hydrology looks at how rainfall becomes runoff across a catchment, and hydraulics looks at how that water moves through pipes, channels and floodways. Together they show how a site will handle storms, so drainage can be designed to protect both the development and the land downstream.

Graphical visualisation

Large datasets are hard to read as tables. I use graphs and maps to show the result first and the detail second.
