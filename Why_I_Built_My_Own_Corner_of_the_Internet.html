Why I Built My Own Corner of the Internet?

We live in an interesting time. Artificial intelligence can write an article, generate an image, compose music or produce an argument in a matter of seconds. Almost anything can now be created with a simple prompt. So, in a world where AI can create almost anything, why am I here writing?

Human civilisation did not reach its present state overnight. It took countless generations of struggle, curiosity, experimentation and learning. Early humans had limited knowledge of the world around them, but through observation, experience, trial and error, they gradually discovered ways to survive and prosper.

I am here to put my thoughts, opinions and perspectives into my own little corner of the internet. Perhaps it is a challenge to myself, and perhaps a challenge to anyone who comes across my writing. I want to think independently, question things, explore ideas and express my views. I don’t necessarily expect everyone to agree with me. In fact, disagreement and constructive criticism are part of what makes an exchange of ideas worthwhile. Human civilisation did not reach its present state overnight. It took countless generations of struggle, curiosity, experimentation and learning. Early humans had limited knowledge of the world around them, but through observation, experience, trial and error, they gradually discovered ways to survive and prosper. Scientists made remarkable discoveries and expanded our understanding of the natural world. Philosophers challenged established beliefs and questioned what was right and wrong. Sociologists examined how societies function, evolve and hold together. Countless others contributed in ways that shaped the world we live in today. Progress was never the achievement of a single individual. It was built upon the accumulated knowledge, experiences, disagreements and contributions of generations.

So, coming to the point, I want to present subjects from a human perspective, always mindful that another human being will eventually read and reflect on them. I want to approach different subjects with respect for all those individuals throughout human civilisation who have contributed to shaping our society, advancing science, developing moral values and bringing humanity to where it stands today. My intention is not necessarily to offer definitive answers, but to explore ideas, question perspectives and share thoughts while acknowledging the enormous contributions of those who came before us. My intention is also not to deny the presense of AI without which this interaction wouldn't be possible.
